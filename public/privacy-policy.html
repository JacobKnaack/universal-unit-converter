Privacy Policy for Universal Unit Converter

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Welcome to Universal Unit Converter (the "Extension"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of our software. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and storage.

1. Information We Collect

Universal Unit Converter is designed as a local browser utility.

  • No Personal Data Collection:

    We do not collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or IP address.

  • No Browsing History Tracking:

    While the Extension requires permission to read web page content to perform its core functions (such as automatic data or text conversion), this processing happens completely locally on your device. Your browsing history, webpage URLs, and page content are never sent to external servers or third parties.

2 Permissions Used and Why

To function correctly, the Extension requests specific browser permissions:

  • Storage:

    Used solely on your local device to save and persist your user preferences (such as configuration toggles and default settings) across browser sessions.

  • Host Permissions / Webpage Access:

    Used exclusively to run local content scripts that parse and automatically update content on the active webpage you are viewing.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Sharing

Because all core operations are executed locally within your browser:

  • Your custom settings are stored securely via the browser's local storage API.

  • No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or external entities.

4. Compliance with Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Our Extension does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from anyone under the age of 13. Since we collect zero data from all users, we are fully compliant with the requirements of COPPA.

5. Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any updates.
